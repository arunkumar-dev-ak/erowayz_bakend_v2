Erowayz Product Specification

Master Features Workflow & UI Guide

A simple, non-technical visual walkthrough of all 19 features: Where they appear on the screens and how they work step-by-step.

🔍
01

Customer Trust Badge (Admin-Configured Threshold)

🏪 Vendor Section (All Vendors) 💻 Admin Panel
📍 Where It Appears in UI
• Vendor Section (All 3 Categories): All vendors across Street Food, Common Shops, and Vehicle Services are notified and see the vibrant green "Trusted Customer" badge next to the customer name on cash orders.
• Admin Panel: Trust Settings (Admin sets target order threshold, e.g. 15 orders) & Customer Profiles (displays total cumulative completed orders).
⚡ How It Works (Simple Workflow)
1
⚙️ Admin-Configured Order Target (No Expiry) Permanent Trust

Admin configures the required order count in the Admin Panel (e.g. 15 completed orders). There is no monthly reset and no expiry limit — orders accumulate continuously!

🛠️ Admin Sets Count ♾️ No Expiry / No Reset ✅ Permanent Badge
2
Groups Across All 3 Categories: Verified Cash on Delivery (COD) purchases across Street Food, Common Shops, and Vehicle Services all count together towards the Admin-set target.
3
All Vendors Notified:
• When a customer reaches the target count, all vendors across all 3 categories see and are notified of the trust status:
🛡️ TRUSTED CUSTOMER (If target not yet reached, no negative badge is shown — leaves customer name normal & simple).
• Admin sees: The exact total completed order count in the customer's Admin profile.
💡
Why It Helps: Groups customer activity across food, shops, and vehicle services to give all vendors a unified, platform-wide trust score against ghost orders.
02

Vendor Automatic Stock Alerts

🏪 Vendor Section 💻 Admin Panel
📍 Where It Appears in UI
• Vendor Dashboard: Top Alert Stack (shows up to 5 stacked low-stock cards).
• Dedicated Alert Log Page: Tapping the stack opens the full alert log page with all notifications list and restock options.
• Admin Panel: Stock Alert Settings (Admin customizes threshold counts and alert badge colors).
(Note: Completely hidden from customer view).
⚡ How It Works (Simple Workflow)
1
Admin Customization: Admin configures threshold counts (e.g., 5, 3, 2, 1) and alert colors from the Admin Panel.
2
Dashboard Stack UI: Alerts appear as a clean stack of up to 5 alerts on top of the Vendor Dashboard as stock decreases.
3
Dedicated Notification Log Page: Clicking the stack opens the dedicated Stock Alert Log Page showing the complete list of notifications and low-stock items (e.g., Existing 30 + 10 New = 40).
4
Auto Out-of-Stock: At 0 units, the system automatically flips the product status to "OUT OF STOCK" to prevent over-ordering.
💡
Why It Helps: Keeps the vendor dashboard clean with a 5-item stack, provides a dedicated notification log page, and allows Admin color customization.
03

Universal Real-Time Notifications

📱 Customer 🏪 Vendor
📍 Where It Appears in UI
• Phone Lock Screen / Push Notification Bar.
• Vendor Section: Dedicated Notification Log Page (lists all past incoming order & booking notifications).
• In-App Auto Refresh when app is open.
⚡ How It Works (Simple Workflow)
1
New Order Alert: Vendor phone rings with a loud sound. Notification shows Customer Name, Items, Amount, and Trust Badge. Tapping it opens the order directly.
2
Customer Updates: Customer hears default notification sound when order is Accepted or Ready.
3
Cancellation Alert: If an order is Cancelled, a special customized alert sound plays to grab immediate attention.
💡
Why It Helps: Vendors never miss an order, have a full notification history log, and customers always know the exact live status of their order.
04

Street Food 20-Min Order Timer

📱 Customer 🏪 Vendor
📍 Where It Appears in UI
• Vendor Section: Dedicated Street Food Order Action Screen (opened directly by clicking the notification).
• Customer Section: Active Order Tracking Screen.
⚡ How It Works (Simple Workflow)
1
Notification Click ➔ Dedicated Page: Clicking the Street Food order notification opens a dedicated action page exclusively for the vendor to Accept or Decline.
2
20-Minute Countdown: When vendor accepts, a 20:00 countdown timer starts ticking on the screen for the first 5 active orders.
3
Vendor Cancel Button (COD): If 20 minutes pass and the Cash on Delivery (COD) customer has not collected the food, a "Cancel Order" button unlocks on the vendor's dedicated screen so they can safely void it.
💡
Why It Helps: Vendor gets a dedicated action page directly from the notification, clear 20-min timing, and a safe cancel option for abandoned COD orders.

Banner Views, Likes & Dislikes

📱 Customer 🏪 Vendor
📍 Where It Appears in UI
Customer Section ➔ Home Promotional Banners.
Vendor Section ➔ My Offer Banners.
⚡ How It Works (Simple Workflow)

5-Parameter Vendor Rating & Reviews

📱 Customer 🏪 Vendor
📍 Where It Appears in UI
Customer Section ➔ Order History (Rate Vendor modal).
Vendor Profile / Shop Info Page ➔ Overall Star Rating & Count.
⚡ How It Works (Simple Workflow)
1
Customer can rate only after completing an order or booking. Exactly 1 review per User (a customer can only review a vendor once, not on every transaction).
2
Tailored 5-Parameter Rating (1 to 5 Stars):
🍔 1. Street Food Vendors:
1. Hygiene & Cleanliness  •  2. Vendor Hospitality  •  3. Serving Speed  •  4. Food Taste  •  5. Value for Money
🛍️ 2. Common Shop Vendors:
1. Product Quality  •  2. Product Availability  •  3. Vendor Behaviour  •  4. Price & Value for Money  •  5. Customer Service
🔧 3. Vehicle & Service Vendors:
1. Service Quality  •  2. Service Speed  •  3. Technician Behaviour  •  4. Pricing & Value for Money  •  5. Service Reliability
3
System averages the 5 scores and shows the final score (e.g. ★ 4.8 from 142 reviews) on the Vendor's public profile.
💡
Why It Helps: Genuine, verified reviews build immense customer trust and reward hardworking vendors.
07

Street Food Product Offer Banner

📱 Customer 🏪 Vendor
📍 Where It Appears in UI
Customer Section ➔ Top of Home screen Carousel.
Vendor Section ➔ Create Offer Banner screen.
⚡ How It Works (Simple Workflow)
1
Vendor uploads a product photo, special offer price, stock quantity, start/end time, and sets a Max Quantity Limit (e.g. max 2 or 3 units per customer to prevent hoarding). (Open to all food vendors).
2
Customer sees the banner, taps it, and books directly or adds to cart. Customer quantity is capped at the Max Quantity Limit, and discount applies automatically.
3
As customers buy, stock auto-decrements. When stock hits 0, it displays "OUT OF STOCK" and disables bookings.
💡
Why It Helps: Street food vendors can run flash sales during special lunch/evening hours to clear items fast.

11

Dynamic Business Load Indicator

📱 Customer 🏪 Vendor 💻 Admin Panel
📍 Where It Appears in UI
• Customer Section: Vendor Profile, Shop Listing Card & Checkout Page.
• Vendor Section: Live Busy Status Badge on Vendor Dashboard.
• Admin Panel: Subscription Settings (Admin sets threshold limits & vendor customization permissions).
⚡ How It Works (Simple Workflow)
1
Admin Configuration: Admin controls the maximum order threshold (e.g. 5 orders = BUSY) and whether vendors are allowed to customize their own lower threshold.
2
Live Workload Counting: System tracks active accepted orders being cooked. Displays a dynamic color-coded status badge:
0: Normal
1: Light
2: Moderate
3: Busy
4: Heavy
5+: MAX BUSY
3
Auto Cool-Down: As orders finish, the color badge automatically cools down back to green (OPEN).
💡
Why It Helps: Admin controls workload limits centrally, while customers see live kitchen rush before ordering to set realistic delivery expectations.

14

Customer Report & Resolution Tracking

📱 Customer 💻 Admin Panel
📍 Where It Appears in UI
• Customer Section: Directly below that specific Order, Booking, or Service screen (Inline Report button & live status — no separate history page).
• Admin Panel: Customer Concerns Dashboard (with Accept, Decline, Mark as Viewed, and Resolved buttons).
(Note: Completely excluded from Vendor Section; vendors do not receive or manage reports).
⚡ How It Works (Simple Workflow)
1
Inline Report Submission: Right below any Order, Shop Booking, or Service screen, customer taps "Report" to submit their concern directly on that transaction.
2
Admin Actions (Accept / Decline / Mark as Viewed / Resolved): All submitted concerns appear in the Admin Panel with action buttons to Accept, Decline, Mark as Viewed, or mark as Resolved.
3
Live Status on Order Screen: When Admin reviews, status updates to "Marked as Seen by Admin". Once resolved, it reflects as "Resolved by Admin".
💡
Why It Helps: Simple inline reporting without cluttered extra pages, gives Admin central control, and keeps vendor workflow clean and unaffected.

Universal Shop QR Code

📱 Customer 🏪 Vendor 💻 Admin Panel
📍 Where It Appears in UI
• Vendor Section & Counter: Printed Standee at Shop Counter / Vendor Section ➔ "Download My QR Code".
• Scanning Method: Customer uses their native Phone Camera or Outside Scanner App (e.g. Google Lens) — no in-app scanner needed.
⚡ How It Works (Simple Workflow)
1
Every vendor gets a high-resolution, branded QR code to print and place on their counter/table.
2
Customer walks up and scans the QR code using their regular Phone Camera or outside scanner app (no in-app scanner required).
3
Smart Redirection:
• If App is Installed: Immediately opens that specific vendor's Shop Info & Menu Page directly in the Erowayz app so they can order on the spot.
• If App is NOT Installed: Automatically directs the customer to the Google Play Store / App Store to download the Erowayz app.
💡
Why It Helps: Converts physical walk-in street traffic into digital app customers with zero friction.

Vendor License Expiry Alert & Update

🏪 Vendor Section 💻 Admin Panel
📍 Where It Appears in UI
• Vendor Section: App Launch / Login Screen (Mandatory Alert Modal / Banner) ➔ Opens the Upload License Screen.
• Admin Panel: Vendor Compliance & Document Verification Dashboard.
⚡ How It Works (Simple Workflow)
1
Login Expiry Check: When a vendor opens and logs into the app, the system automatically checks the expiry date of their business license (e.g. FSSAI / Trade License).
2
Intimation Alert: If the license is expired, the app immediately displays a prominent alert: "Your Business License has expired. Please update your license document to continue receiving orders."
3
Tap to Update & Admin Approval: Tapping the alert directly opens the License Update Screen to upload the renewed document. Admin reviews and approves it in the Admin Panel.
💡
Why It Helps: Keeps all active vendors 100% legally certified and protected without manual phone calls or office tracking.
